# Architecture audit, 27 Nov 2026

This audit was done after the first decisive training attempt, and **before any further training
run or any Amendment 2/3 call**. Every change below is committed publicly before the runs it
affects, as the OSF registration requires (osf.io/4xmq7: "any change to the reward or training
configuration after the first runs is made by a dated amendment before further runs").

## Critical: would have produced wrong or no results

| # | finding | effect | fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | The reward was called with **one candidate count K for the whole batch**. 29 of the 2,000 real training cases have 9 candidates, not 10. | The label-free real-case arm (H5) would crash (or mis-score) in about 1 step in 5. | Each patient group carries its own K (`PatientGroup.K`). For the batch-level M, patient means are zero-padded, which leaves every entropy unchanged. Tested (`tests/test_audit_fixes.py`). |
| C2 | The **collapse safeguard compared a single step's M with a 5-step mean**. M is a 16-patient batch statistic with a step-to-step CV of 0.16–0.26. | Both decisive runs stopped at steps 10 and 13 with no sign of collapse. RLVR, which does not optimise M, tripped it too. | The mean of the last 20 steps is compared with the mean of the first 10, at the same 30% threshold. A stationary run never triggers it; a real collapse does within 20 steps (tested). Both runs are reported in `docs/training_runs.md` and rerun from scratch. |
| C3 | **The registered confirmatory tests were not implemented as registered.** `ccrl train compare` gives two-sided paired differences. It has no H2 relative-reduction test with a 25% threshold, no Holm correction, no H4 ratio, no H6 one-sided Holm test or panel median, no H7 closure ratio, and no pooling over seeds. | The confirmatory analysis could not have been run as registered. | `src/ccrl/train/hypotheses.py` and `ccrl train hypotheses` implement H2–H7 exactly as worded in the registration, before any training result exists. Tested on synthetic data with known answers. |
| C4 | **`tau_I` was hard-coded to 0.10** in `ccr_only`, `ccr_acc` and `labelfree_mcr`. Only a command-line flag carried the measured value (0.788). | Any run started without the flag would silently use the wrong registered constant. | The measured value is in `config/train/base.yaml`, and the hard-coded values are removed. Every arm resolves to 0.788 (checked). |

## Important: biased or noisier than necessary

| # | finding | fix |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | The reduced-evidence (x-) rollouts' invariance term compared each rollout with a mean that **included itself**. With H = 2, that roughly halves its measured inconsistency. | A leave-one-out mean, as already used for the noise floor. The synthetic-policy suite still passes 10/10. |
| I2 | **Advantages were centred on the whole paired group.** The x- rollouts are less accurate by construction, so their advantage was always negative. That is unbiased in expectation, but high-variance: with 2 rollouts per patient it pushes the policy away from whatever it answers on reduced evidence. | Each rendering (x, x', x-) is centred on its own mean (a per-prompt baseline, as in GRPO), with one pooled SD (`advantage_baseline: rendering`). The objective and the reward are unchanged. |
| I3 | **H6 is confounded by decoding.** Trained models are evaluated at temperature 0.7 (registered), panel models at provider default, so a retest-flip comparison partly measures temperature. | Each trained model is also evaluated at temperature 0, and compared with the panel's temperature-0 arms (Amendment 2). This is reported as a secondary analysis; the registered comparison is unchanged. |
| I4 | **"Toy dataset" risk.** Paper 2's main comparisons (H2, H3, H6) are on DDXPlus, which is synthetic. | Every trained model is evaluated on the post-freeze real case reports (Amendment 3), alongside the registered real-case tests (H4, H5). Amendment 3 now uses every passing November case (up to 238), not 150. |

## Limitations to state (not fixable within this study)

- **The untrained Qwen3-4B is at chance on real case reports** (top-1 0.107 with 10 candidates,
  thinking off). Its answers are not degenerate. Real-case claims for the trained 4B rest on
  stability and invariance, and on top-3 (H5), not on top-1. Thinking mode would raise accuracy
  but multiply the training cost about 5×.
- **Kimi K3 prepared the real cases and is also a panel model.** It extracted the findings and
  distractors; GLM-5, a different vendor, verified them. Kimi K3's lead over the next model is
  larger on real cases (+0.20 top-1) than on DDXPlus (+0.10). That fits harder cases, but
  self-preference cannot be excluded. A robustness option is to re-prepare a random subset with
  GLM-5 as the extractor.
- **DDXPlus has been public since 2022.** Its patients are generated and our renderings are new
  text, so verbatim contamination is unlikely, but the dataset's structure could be familiar.
  The post-freeze real cases are the clean check.
- **The panel's provider-default temperature is not disclosed by Bedrock.** The temperature-0
  arms bound its effect.

## Checked and found sound

- **No case overlap between training and evaluation:**
  - DDXPlus training pool and distillation from the train split, the panel from the test split;
  - MedCaseReasoning training from its train split, the panel from its test split;
  - held-out pathologies excluded from training and from distillation (0 of 2,000);
  - pilot, panel and replication cases disjoint;
  - the November cases share no PMCID with MedCaseReasoning.
- **Evaluation seeds:** the base and retest items have different IDs and therefore independent
  sampling seeds, so retest noise is real noise.
- **Reward:** the synthetic-policy suite passes 10/10 after the fixes, including the
  non-identifiability result (Proposition 2) and the noise-floor correction.
- **The vLLM rollout engine agrees with the training model** (mean importance weight 1.000), with
  0% invalid answers.
- **Freezes:** every input of the earlier jobs is unchanged, and the item files of Amendment 3
  live outside the main freeze.
